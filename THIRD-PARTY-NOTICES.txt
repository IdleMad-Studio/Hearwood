PART 1. MODEL AND VOICES
------------------------

1. Pocket TTS (english_2026-10 checkpoint)
   By Kyutai. https://kyutai.org/
   Source: https://huggingface.co/kyutai/pocket-tts-without-voice-cloning
           (commit a7056f505a4a8ca72d8b5fb1440071d6f1a35739)
   Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
            https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode
   Included in the Microsoft Store package, already converted (see Changes).
   Other builds don't include it: they download it from Hugging Face the
   first time they run, at the pinned commit above, and check each file's
   SHA-256.
   Changes: Hearwood converts the downloaded files for the Phonon runtime. It
   moves the BOS embedding out of the weights file and writes a runtime
   config. The Store package carries the converted files; other builds
   convert them on the user's PC.

   Kyutai's terms prohibit voice impersonation or cloning without explicit and
   lawful consent, presenting generated speech as genuine recordings of real
   people or events, and other illegal, harmful or deceptive use.

2. Built-in voices (assets/voices/*.safetensors, included in Hearwood's builds)
   Each voice is a blend of two voices of Kokoro-82M, by hexgrad
   (https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M, Apache License 2.0,
   https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). Kokoro read a short passage in
   each blend; that recording was then turned into a Pocket TTS voice
   embedding with the speaker encoder from KevinAHM's ONNX export of Pocket TTS
   (https://huggingface.co/KevinAHM/pocket-tts-onnx, commit
   58a6d00cf13d239b6748cb0769f35c580a8f606c, CC BY 4.0) and moved onto the
   english_2026-10 checkpoint. Neither Kokoro nor the encoder is included in
   Hearwood.
   Each voice mixes two Kokoro voices (50/50 or 60/40), so none is a copy of
   a single Kokoro voice. The blend recipes are in voices/Voice mixes.txt in
   Hearwood's source.

3. xn-ptts (Phonon runtime), built as ptts_ffi.dll
   By Gradium. https://github.com/gradium-ai/xn-ptts
   (commit ee395caac058950cbcabfa3785c63630942f31ed)

   Changes: tools/build-phonon.ps1 patches ptts/src/plan.rs to generate one
